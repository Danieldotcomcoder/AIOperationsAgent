# Delayed shipment escalation

Synthetic demonstration policy. Not an actual distributor's operating policy.

## Carrier delays

When a shipment is past its estimated delivery date and has no confirmed delivery,
contact the carrier for a current delivery estimate. Record the response and notify
the customer of the confirmed estimate. Do not promise an unconfirmed delivery date.

## Supervisor follow-up

If the carrier has not responded within two business days, propose a supervisor
follow-up task. A supervisor must approve the proposed task before it is created.

## Missing evidence

If shipment records are incomplete, state which records are missing. Do not assume
that an order shipped or arrived without the corresponding operational evidence.
